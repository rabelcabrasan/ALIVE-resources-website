About This Site

Why this site exists and who it's for.

This site shares free Arabic Language and Islamic Values Education (ALIVE) resources created for teachers, students, and parents. Everything here — books, lesson presentations, worksheets, and tools — is free to download and use.

These materials are developed and maintained by Teacher Rabel D. Cabrasan, an ALIVE Teacher at F. Dela Cruz - P. Sagun Elementary School (School ID 128785), using references from the DepEd Tagum City Division.

What's Available

  • Books — ALIVE Arabic Language and Islamic Values books in PDF.
  • PPTs & Worksheets — Lesson presentations (ILAW format), Budget of Work, and student worksheets for Grades 1–6.
  • Attendance System — A desktop app for QR-based attendance, assessment records, and certificate generation.
  • ALIVE Books App — An Android app for browsing the books on a phone, tablet, or TV.
